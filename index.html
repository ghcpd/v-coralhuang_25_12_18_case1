📋 Task Dashboard

Manage, search, filter, and update your tasks with ease

Guide

Search Tasks

Type in the search box to find tasks by title. Matching keywords are highlighted in yellow.

Filter & Sort

Filter by Status: Select from the dropdown (All, TODO, IN_PROGRESS, DONE)

Sort: Click any column header to sort. Click again to reverse. Click once more to clear sorting.

Pagination

Choose items per page, then use Prev/Next buttons to navigate. Current page is shown on the right.

Update Status

Click the Next Status button on any row to advance: TODO → IN_PROGRESS → DONE. Updated rows briefly highlight in yellow.

Reset

Click Reset to clear all filters, sorts, and search, returning to page 1.
